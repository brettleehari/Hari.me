What I saw, 2023

A weekend project. A classifier in front of three endpoints, because we were rate-limited. “Why would anyone pay five percent for this.”

What they saw

Every app will talk to many models. Nobody wants N keys and N bills. And the map of who uses which model is worth more than the fee.

I had the same model, the same problem and the same tools. I had the wrong problem statement: I solved my rate limit. They solved everyone’s. Deterministic software, read backwards. openrouter.ai
