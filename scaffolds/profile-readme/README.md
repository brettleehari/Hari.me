# Hariprasad Sudharshan

Building AI products that work in production. Open source since 2016, research since 2017, shipping since 2019.

---

I build the full stack around the model — the product layer that compensates for what the model can't do on its own. Prompt design as defensive programming. Context management as infrastructure. Evaluation as a first-class concern, not an afterthought. The customer interacts with the product, not the model, and that distinction drives every design decision I make.

The arc: I started contributing to open source in 2016, published research and filed patents starting in 2017, built my first ML product in 2019, and have been shipping enterprise AI products since. Before AI, I spent 18 years designing internal developer platforms at scale — CI/CD, test automation, defect tracking, measurement frameworks. Every integration problem I solved in platform engineering showed up again when AI arrived. The scars transferred.

I'm looking for AI product management and strategy roles where AI is the core business, not a bolt-on feature — teams that need someone who understands both the model's capabilities and the enterprise constraints that determine whether it actually ships.
